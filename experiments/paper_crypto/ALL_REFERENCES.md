# Complete reference list — every paper cited across this project

40 unique works. Two papers were produced: the **ICDEC crypto study** (22 refs) and the
**PINN architecture paper** (25 refs); they share 7 references.

**Verification status.** Entries marked **[V]** were verified live against the publisher
during this session — title, venue, volume, pages and DOI all confirmed. Entries marked
**[S]** are standard, heavily-cited works whose details are well established but which I did
**not** re-verify this session. Spot-check the [S] DOIs before submitting; a wrong DOI is
worse than none.
